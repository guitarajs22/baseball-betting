Betting Thresholds
%
The minimum edge your simulation must show over the book's implied probability before a bet is recommended. The threshold odds shown on each game are calculated from this number.
Default: 6%. Raising this gives fewer but higher-confidence picks. Lowering it gives more picks but more noise.
× full Kelly
Fraction of the full Kelly bet to use. Full Kelly (1.0) maximises long-run growth but has high variance.
Default: 0.25 (quarter Kelly) — a conservative approach that limits drawdown while still growing the bankroll.

How "Bet At" Prices Work

After every simulation the app converts each win probability into a threshold price — the worst odds you should accept to still have your configured edge.

Example: The sim says the Under hits 55% of the time. With a 6% edge requirement, you need the book to imply ≤49% — which is +104 or better. If the book is offering -108, that's only implied 52%, so you'd pass.

"Or better" always means a more favourable price for you:

  • Underdog bets: a higher positive number (+120 is better than +104)
  • Favourite bets: a less negative number (-108 is better than -115)

The Kelly Bet shown is the dollar amount the Kelly Criterion suggests wagering based on your current bankroll and the edge on offer. Sticking to the suggested sizes keeps risk of ruin very low even over a long season.
